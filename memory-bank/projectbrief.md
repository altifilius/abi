# Abi — Project Brief

- Goal: Provide Turkish SMEs and startups with an AI grant copilot that turns rough R&D ideas into fundable, compliant submissions.
- Core outcomes: generate a concise project 1-pager, assess eligibility against local programs (TÜBİTAK, KOSGEB, EU), and guide documents/incorporation steps.
- Audience: founders and ops leads seeking public grants without in-house grant expertise.
- Scope (current): authenticated, persisted PostgreSQL workspaces with Free project profiles/drafts/exports and source-backed funding discovery; Pro document/OCR, application preparation and team workflows. Canonical development/runtime directory: `/home/emre/Projects/abi`.
- Success signals: users reach a draft 1-pager, see ranked fund matches with rationale, and understand which documents/steps remain.
