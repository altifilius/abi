# Abi — Project Brief

- Goal: Provide Turkish SMEs and startups with an AI grant copilot that turns rough R&D ideas into fundable, compliant submissions.
- Core outcomes: clarify an idea in conversation, confirm project scope/country, develop five template-based reports with isolated AI sessions, discover source-backed domestic/international support and prepare reviewed application documents.
- Audience: founders and ops leads seeking public grants without in-house grant expertise.
- Scope (current): authenticated, persisted PostgreSQL workspaces with Free project profiles/drafts/exports and source-backed funding discovery; Pro document/OCR, application preparation and team workflows. Canonical development/runtime directory: `/home/emre/Projects/abi`.
- Success signals: the user always sees one clear next action, can discuss and apply one report section at a time, retains manual edits and earlier records, and understands source/eligibility/application boundaries without an overloaded menu.
